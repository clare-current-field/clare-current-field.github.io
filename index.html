About

Design that starts with how you want to live.

Current Field was founded by Chuan — a designer who believes that truly beautiful spaces are also deeply livable. Not just photogenic, but functional, personal, and easy to maintain. Spaces that make you want to come home.

The studio's name holds two meanings: 川, the Chinese character for river — Chuan's given name — and current field, the physics term for a magnetic force that is invisible, stable, and precisely directed. That duality — intuitive feeling grounded in rigorous thinking — is at the heart of how we work.

With a background in structural and civil engineering, nine years leading product at technology companies, and over a decade of hands-on residential design, Chuan brings a rare combination to each project: the eye of someone who has spent years studying beauty, and the mind of someone who knows how buildings actually work.

We work with homeowners across the Bay Area who care deeply about their environment — people who want a space that reflects them, executed with precision and delivered without drama.

B.S. & M.S., Civil & Structural Engineering — Stanford University Certificate, Interior Design — New York School of Interior Design Residential projects in Los Altos, Palo Alto, and surrounding Bay Area communities
Services

Two ways to work together.

Full-Service Design

For renovations and new builds — from structural decisions through the final arrangement of objects. We manage the complexity so you don't have to.

  • Space planning and layout
  • Architectural plan consultation
  • Hard material selection — tile, stone, cabinetry, flooring
  • In-person sourcing at showrooms and trade vendors
  • Budget planning and tracking
  • Timeline and procurement management
  • Contractor and architect coordination
  • Soft furnishing and styling to completion

Soft Furnishing & Styling

For spaces that are structurally complete but need to come alive. We bring the furniture, the objects, and the logic of living.

  • Space measurement and furniture layout
  • Moodboard and concept development
  • Furniture and textile selection
  • Art sourcing and placement
  • Storage and organization planning
  • Installation and styling
  • Custom art direction (original works sourced or commissioned)
We work with residential projects up to 4,000 sq ft within a 30-minute radius of Los Altos, CA. We do not provide architectural stamped drawings or structural engineering services. 3D renderings are not currently part of our standard offering — we present work through moodboards and material samples.
